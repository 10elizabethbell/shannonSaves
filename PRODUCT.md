# PRODUCT — ShannonSaves

## Platform
web

## Stack
static single-file HTML/CSS/JS, hosted on GitHub Pages

## Users
- Primary: people looking for a home cleaner, opening the link on a phone inside Facebook's in-app browser (the business is found through a Burlington County NJ Facebook group post).
- Secondary: Shannon, the owner, who receives the site for free.

## Purpose
Give a one-person home cleaning business its first web presence: show who Shannon is, what she cleans, and make it effortless to ask for the free quote her flyer offers.

## Positioning
- Business name as printed on the flyer: "ShannonSaves" (the flyer letters it "$hannon$aves", dollar signs as the S's).
- Tagline (verbatim): "Peace of mind, Time & Money".
- Services (verbatim): "Reliable Deep Cleaning, Bi-Weekly Home Maintenance and Organization".
- Caption (verbatim): "If anyone is looking for reliable and trustworthy home cleaning….. Reach out today".
- Offer (verbatim): "Reach Out Today for a Free Quote".

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone (609) 784-4976; whether it takes texts is unknown. Email Shannonmac66@gmail.com (footer only).
- No booking system, no Facebook business page (only a personal profile), no reviews, no prices, no hours, no service area published.
- Do not publish a location (brief: the 609 area code and group placement are not statements of service area).

## Brand commitments
- Flyer colors: navy ground (~#0e3b66), lime-green display lettering, cream ring and phone/email text, pale grey-blue dots (bubble-like circles).
- Shannon's own portrait is on her flyer (inferred: she is the owner and the person customers deal with).
- Tone: plain, trust-focused ("reliable and trustworthy").

## Evidence on hand
- One flyer image (1050x600) with her portrait. No work photos, no before/afters, no reviews.

## Principles
- Speak as Shannon, first person ("I").
- Only facts from the flyer and post; no invented prices, reviews, years, towns.
- Phone first; the quote request is the primary action.
